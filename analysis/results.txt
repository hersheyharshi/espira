1. HOW FAR DOES THE FIELD REACH?  (Debye length)
========================================================================
  LEO 400-550 km, dense (day)        lambda_D =     0.0033 m
  LEO 400-550 km, sparse (night)     lambda_D =     0.0235 m
  GEO quiet                          lambda_D =   235.0924 m
  GEO storm (hot, thin)              lambda_D =  2350.9235 m
  -> In LEO the plasma screens charge within millimetres-centimetres;
     in GEO the field reaches hundreds of metres. LEO is NOT a vacuum for electrostatics.

3. IS THE FORCE ENOUGH?  (delta-v budget, best case = vacuum force at 10 m)
========================================================================
  Push 100 kg debris for 1 hour  : delta-v = 7.974e-03 m/s
  Push 100 kg debris for 1 day   : delta-v = 1.914e-01 m/s
  Push 100 kg debris for 30 days : delta-v = 5.741e+00 m/s
  Stop a crossing fragment (relative speed)                      needs  10000.0 m/s ->     143.05 years of continuous push
  Lower 550 km circular orbit to fast-decay perigee (~200 km)    needs    100.0 m/s ->       1.43 years of continuous push
  GEO graveyard re-orbit (+300 km)                               needs     11.0 m/s ->       0.16 years of continuous push
  -> Even ignoring plasma screening, electrostatic force at 10 m is ~mN-scale:
     useful for slow, co-orbital nudging/detumbling (GEO tractor concept),
     hopeless for capturing objects that are moving relative to the probe.
